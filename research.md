  <section>
    <h2>Simulation, Verification &amp; Validation</h2>
    <p>I study how modeling assumptions, numerical choices, and empirical calibration influence the fidelity of microscopic traffic simulations. My ANNSIM 2026 paper evaluates lane-level flow and speed against California PeMS detector measurements and demonstrates why corridor averages alone are insufficient for validation.</p>
    <p>Ongoing directions include efficient verification and validation, calibration, and comparisons across levels of simulation abstraction. I contribute simulation and validation capabilities to the <a href="https://github.com/scalation/scalation_2.0">ScalaTion</a> framework.</p>
  </section>

  <section>
    <h2>Time-Series Foundation Models &amp; Model Monitoring</h2>
    <p>I evaluate pretrained forecasting models under distribution shift and investigate when common monitoring signals are reliable. In spacecraft telemetry, I compare how drift severity relates to forecasting error for per-channel LSTMs and zero-shot foundation models, including Chronos-2 and TimesFM.</p>
  </section>

  <section>
    <h2>Robust and Reliable Machine Learning</h2>
    <p>My collaborative research includes robust causal graph neural networks under compound sensor corruption and selective classification of glycan structures under distribution shift. These projects share an emphasis on measuring failure modes rather than relying solely on aggregate accuracy.</p>
  </section>

  <section>
    <h2>AI-Assisted Simulation and Evaluation</h2>
    <p>I am exploring how structured scenario specifications, formal validation, runtime checks, and reproducible experiment workflows can support the evaluation of AI-generated simulation artifacts. These are research directions, not claims of a deployed autonomous system.</p>
  </section>
